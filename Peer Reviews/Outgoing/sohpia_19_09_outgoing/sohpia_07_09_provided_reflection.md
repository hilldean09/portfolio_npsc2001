# The Copied Reflection: AA Accredited training

To be able to gain unsupervised access to the PC2 lab in building 304, where I am conducting my research for my project, I had to complete the Approved Arrangements accredited training. This training included two modules and took an overall combined time of 5 hours to complete. This training is a course that allows me to handle goods that have been imported into Australia or Western Australia, or quarantine materials. 
 
Even though my project does not use government regulated materials, it was important for me to do this training before I could have access to the lab on my own. This is because many regulated, imported materials are used in this lab by others so everyone working in the lab must know what to do in the case this material is becomes uncontained in the lab. Before doing this training I would not have known how to safety contain any biological matter that was out of place so by completing it, I have made the lab a safer place for myself and everyone around me. It is a legal requirement for anyone who is working unsupervised with access to regulated materials to have completed this training. If I had not done it I would have needed supervision for every experiment I do which may have inhibited my learning by not allowing me to solve problems on my own. Overall, I think this experience has helped me to improve my skills of problem identification and solution, as it has given me more knowledge that I can use during my research to identify and fix problems I come across. 
 
If I continue with this project into the next few years, I may have to work with material that is regulated by the government. If this is the case I will already be prepared and armed with the knowledge of how to safely handle imported and quarantined material and the legal qualifications to do this.
