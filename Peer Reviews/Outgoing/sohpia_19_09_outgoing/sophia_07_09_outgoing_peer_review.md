# My Peer Review

I found your reflection on the Approved Arrangements accredited training to be very educational and thoroughly explained its purpose, tangible benefits, and intangible benefits in relation to your project. I found your connection to the intangible benefits of the AA accredited training to be particularly interesting and showcased your deeper reasoning about the process.

Your reflection clearly reflects on the alternative case (i.e. not having completed the training) and its effects on your project and learning. Additionally, you make a very specific connection to the future applicability of the training (i.e. continuing the project in coming years). Your reflection showcases a clean personal, introspective, and thorough writing style that moves through the ideas naturally.

I believe your reflection could benefit from including explicit references to artefacts and Independent Study Contract (ISC) achievement outcomes. A concrete artefact relating to your AA accredited training (e.g. notes, a work sample. a certificate) would give the reader more insight into the process and/or a material connection to ground their interpretation with. Additionally, reflecting on how the training relates to your ISC achievement outcomes may amplify how the reader understands the impact this has on your professional growth by demonstrating that it was already a target point. Finally, while you made a short-term connection between the AA accredited training and your future endeavours, your reflection may benefit from an additional long-term connection to its benefit through your desired career.

Thank you very much for sharing your reflection. I believe it was very well thought out and written and I found it very informative.
