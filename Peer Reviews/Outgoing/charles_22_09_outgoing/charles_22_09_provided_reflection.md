# The Copied Reflection

Recently I finished recording and measuring the data for the radial growth experiment. The data was plotted using R, a scripting software specializing in processing and graphing large datasets such as those used in molecular biology. I first wrote a script that plotted each data group individually, however I wished to update this to read and plot every data group automatically. I had to ask for help with this task as I am not very experienced in computer science. This came with its own issue. In a previous reflection I had stated that I did not have an issue with articulating problems related to molecular biology, and although that does hold true for mol gen I do face a complication with articulating problems in other disciplines. I asked several other advanced science students with knowledge in comp sci for assistance but due to my inexperience I struggled to explain what I wanted to do. Eventually they understood and tried to help (to no avail, R isn't commonly used outside of biology) but this experience has awoken me to the fact that interdisciplinary communication is an important skill that I should work on to be successful in the future.
