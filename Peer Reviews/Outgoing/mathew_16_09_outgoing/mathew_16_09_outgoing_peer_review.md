# My Peer Review

I found your reflection to be a very concise and comprehensive overview of the non-content-specific aspects of the Numerical Physics Modules. A strong point of your reflection was naturally and distinctly covering a variety of areas of personal growth (programming/technical knowledge, understanding theory vs application, and teamwork). Your writing feels very polished and does not strike me as either very informal or very formal, a solid balance for a reflection I believe.

I believe where your reflection could be most improved is by making clear connections to artefacts as demonstrations of your improvement, having also done the Numerical Physics Modules I would recommend one of the graphs (e.g. optimisation algorithms), animations (e.g. wave propagation), or audio files (e.g. physicist whale calls). On a similar vein, providing a specific example of a problem we encountered as a group (I recall there was one final problem that we had significant trouble with as a group) and resolved over discussion would strengthen your demonstration of personal growth.

I enjoyed reading your reflection and I believe your compartmentalised structure for covering a variety of achievement outcomes is something I can use to improve my own reflections.
