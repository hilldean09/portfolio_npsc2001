# My Peer Review
I believe your reflection clearly tackles a point of personal growth, establishing your past speech preparation methods of verbatim memorisation and comparing the outcomes to your experience with your mid-year presentation method of focusing on better understanding your own project and memorising key points. Your writing style is very personal and incorporates your feelings. However, in regards to the rubric in particular, I believe there are a few discrete areas for improvement.

In your reflection, there are multiple parts of the *Weekly Reflections* section of the *Portfolio* rubric provided in the unit outline that are not addressed. Specifically, no connections to artefacts (e.g. notes on the feedback from the planetary sciences group), ISC achievement outcomes, nor career aspirations/growth are explicitly made. I certainly agree that communication and presentation skills are valuable for any career, however some specific examples of its place in your desired career path would make this reflection more targeted and impactful on me as a reader.

I believe that much of the implicit reasoning and the "why" in your reflection can be easily assumed. I also believe that making explicit inductive connections, even small, between the reasons for making changes (e.g. your preparation style), the changes themselves, and how they caused an effect could elevate your writing style and provide a deeper understanding of your thought process.

I believe that your reflection presents the narrative around your mid-year presentation in a personal style, however I believe that including more explicit reasoning could deepen your reflection and give the reader a more unique insight into your growth. Thank you very much for sharing your reflection, and I do hope that you find the above useful.
