# The Copied Reflection: Mid-year presentation

The mid-year presentation was a little difficult to figure out in the time we had. Because I am doing physics, I only found out what my project was going to be around a week before. This meant I didn't have as much time as I would have liked to prepare my presentation. To try and alleviate this, I prioritised covering the points in the unit outline, to try and make sure I hit all the parts in the marking guide. Unfortunately, this meant that my presentation was short, partly because I was focused on the other points, but also because I wasn't too familiar with the subject material of my project yet. The thing I found the most helpful in preparing for this assessment were the few times I got together with the other physics guys to practice our presentations. For me, this was the first time I had done a presentation like this without a script, so the practice I did I found really useful in working out what to say and when to say some things. It was a bit difficult to adjust to working without a script, so I really needed that practice to get comfortable in my speaking. After viewing other students presentations, I think I figured out what I could have done differently with mine. In the future, I think I would structure the presentation differently. While some of the other sections wouldn't be much changed, I realised there was a lot I could improve in how I presented the actual content of my project. I compressed most of it into one or two slides in the PowerPoint, but it would have been better presented and a lot easier to understand if I spread it out more. This would also have helped me with knowing what to say when, because each slide would be less cluttered and the visual aid would help me know where I was. In the future I think this is how I will approach these sort of assessments, and it is something I plan on implementing in the end of year showcase.
