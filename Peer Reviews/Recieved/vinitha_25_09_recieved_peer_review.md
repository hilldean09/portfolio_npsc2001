For example, if you have since used an LLM for boilerplate, testing, or to interrogate your code while still validating the output yourself, that would provide really strong evidence that the feedback actually changed your workflow. Similarly, if you tried applying the idea of only learning the depth of a topic that is necessary for your current task, reflecting on whether that was difficult or uncomfortable at first would make the reflection more personal and honest. I think that would also make your final conclusion about finding the balance between learning and productivity feel more demonstrated rather than just being a future intention.
