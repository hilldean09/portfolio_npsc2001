# Face Orientation Test Failure Debugging
Investigating the face orientation test failure with the more comprehensive mesh generated by ```t8_cmesh_new_tet_orientation_test()``` function, initially revealed that, seemingly, for non-zero face orientations between a given element, of which we will consider some corner, and the face neighbour corresponding to corner-faces about the aforementioned corner, the neighbour face returned by relevant T8code functions does not include the equivalent neighbour vertex.

Further investigation has revealed that the neighbour element returned by relevant T8code functions doesn't include the equivalent vertex at all.

Viewing the tetrahedral orientation test in paraview reveals that the geometry based testing may not be at all applicable, as the different tetrahedra appear to be connected but geometrically separated (including their vertices).

I will focus on continuing testing via the method of manufactured recommended by Dr. Martin.
